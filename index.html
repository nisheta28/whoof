Our Vision

Pet parents often struggle to find someone they trust with their pet when they travel. They lean on friends, family and WhatsApp groups, or on kennels that can feel impersonal and a little scary.

Whoof is a trust-first pet-sitting network that makes those connections easier to discover and easier to trust.

How much does it cost?

It depends on how long the stay is and what your dog needs. We'll share pricing on our first call, before you commit to anything. The meet & greet is always free.

Which dogs can stay with you?

We welcome most friendly dogs, big or small, indie or pedigree. If your dog is reactive, very young, elderly or needs medication, tell us in the form and we'll chat about whether we're the right fit.

Do you sit cats too?

Right now we focus on dogs. If you have a cat, get in touch anyway, because we're building out our network of hosts.

Do dogs need to be vaccinated?

Yes. We ask that dogs are up to date on core vaccinations (including rabies) and on tick & flea prevention, to keep every guest safe.

What happens in an emergency?

Before the stay, we note your vet's details and an emergency contact. If anything comes up, we call you straight away and take your dog to the nearest vet if needed.

What should I pack?

Their usual food (sudden switches upset tummies), any medication, a leash, and something that smells like home: a favourite toy, blanket or bed.
